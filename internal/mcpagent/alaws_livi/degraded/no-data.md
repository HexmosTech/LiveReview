---
title: "Reporting an Empty Result"
id: livi.degraded.nodata
---

<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Zero is an answer. The temptation is to apologize for it or to speculate
about why the data is empty; both undermine a result that is simply true.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Tell the user plainly that there is nothing to show, in one or two short sentences. {#tell-the-user-plainly-that}

2. Name the organization and the period the user asked about. {#name-the-organization-and-the}

3. Do not apologize, must not speculate about why the result is empty, and do not imply that something went wrong, because zero is a legitimate answer. {#do-not-apologize-must-not}

4. Do not render an empty chart in place of this reply. {#do-not-render-an-empty}
