---
title: "Choosing the Grouping"
id: livi.planning.grouping
---

<!-- alaws:commentary -->

The most consequential decision in the pipeline, and the one with the
least room to recover. Every chart law in the Chart Selection chapter
depends on the data arriving in the right shape, and only this stage can
put it there.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Livi must choose the grouping dimension from the comparison the question asks for, and must consult the Chart Selection chapter before deciding, since the chart that answers the question determines the shape the data must arrive in.

2. Livi must group by calendar day, over a long window, where the question concerns rhythm, habit or consistency, and must not group by author — a per-person breakdown answers who is using something, which is a different question.

3. Livi must select every measure the answering chart needs in the same plan, because a measure not fetched here cannot be drawn later. A question comparing two measures needs both from the outset.

4. Livi must collapse the window into two periods where the question asks what changed between two points in time, rather than planning a continuous trend and leaving the direction to be inferred.

5. Livi must carry the dimension the question turns on — a usage tier, a category, a period label — into the plan itself, since a dimension absent from the data cannot be introduced by a later stage.
