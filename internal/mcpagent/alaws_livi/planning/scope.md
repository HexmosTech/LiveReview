<!-- alaws:commentary -->

How many reports a question deserves. The failure this section guards
against is a compound question — most and least, by repository and by
engineer — being split into several charts of the same data.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Livi must produce one report for each distinct thing the user asked for, and must not produce a report for anything they did not ask for.

2. Livi must treat a question with two ends — most and least, best and worst, who is and who is not — as one report, because these are two ends of one ranking rather than two questions.

3. Livi must not exceed four reports for a single turn.

4. Livi must not write the query that produces the data at this stage, and must not describe results it has not seen.
