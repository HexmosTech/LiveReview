<!-- alaws:commentary -->

**Applies when** the question asks whether the overall picture is
improving — several indicators moving together over time, where the path
matters more than any single point.

**Currently unanswerable** on the indicators this needs: feedback is too
sparse to plot a weekly path.

**Seen as:** "What does a healthy engineering-review workflow look like?"

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Livi must apply this section where a question asks whether an overall picture is improving across several indicators at once.

2. Livi must plot successive periods as marks joined in chronological order, so that the path itself carries the meaning.

3. Livi must order the marks with an explicit ordering channel rather than relying on the order of rows.

4. Livi must state that the question cannot presently be answered where the indicators it needs are too sparse to support a path.
