---

<!-- alaws:commentary -->

How many interpretations to produce depends on the question's specificity.
Broad questions deserve more angles; narrow questions need fewer.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Specific query → 1-2 interpretations. {#specific-query}

2. Broad query → 3-5 interpretations covering different angles. {#broad-query}

3. Never exceed 5. {#never-exceed-5}
