---
title: "Bucket, or Show Every Member"
id: livi.charts.distribution.general
---

<!-- alaws:commentary -->

The rule the rest of this family builds on, and the choice it turns on: whether the shape of the spread or the individuals within it is the answer.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Where a question asks how spread out a measure is across many members of a population, bucket the measure and show the resulting shape. {#where-question-asks-how-spread}

2. Where the population is small enough to show every member and the outliers are the point of the question, show every member instead of bucketing. {#where-the-population-is-small}
