title: "Two Axes Are Required"
id: livi.charts.relationship.general
---

<!-- alaws:commentary -->

The rule the rest of this family builds on. One axis cannot carry a question about how two measures relate, however well the measure is chosen.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Where a question asks how two measures relate rather than how one ranks, render a scatter chart with one mark per entity, a measure on each axis, and size for a third measure where one exists.
