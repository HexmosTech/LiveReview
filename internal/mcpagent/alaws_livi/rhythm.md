A question about habit is a question about the calendar. Who did the work
is a different question, governed by the Ranking chapter.

Zero-filling is not a detail in this chapter, it is the whole chart. The
grid exists to show the weekends, the abandoned fortnight, the day someone
stopped. A missing row draws nothing, so an empty day would appear as
blank paper rather than an empty cell and the signal would vanish.
