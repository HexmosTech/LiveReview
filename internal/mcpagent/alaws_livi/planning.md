<!-- alaws:commentary -->

The second call. It decides what the answer will be made of: how many
reports to produce, what each one counts, and — most consequentially —
along which dimension the data is grouped.

This is where answers are most often lost. The grouping chosen here is
frozen before the Finalizing stage runs: a report grouped by month cannot
later be drawn as a calendar, and one that selected a single measure
cannot later be drawn as a relationship between two. Finalizing can only
present what Planning fetched, so the Chart Selection chapter is sent to
this stage as well.
