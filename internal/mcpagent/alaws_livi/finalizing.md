<!-- alaws:commentary -->

The third call, made once per report and only after the row count is
known. It writes the query that produces the answer and decides how the
answer is presented.

This stage can only present what Planning fetched. Where the data that
arrived cannot answer the question asked, the honest move is to say so
rather than to draw the closest available chart and let it imply an answer
it does not support.
