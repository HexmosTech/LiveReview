<!-- alaws:commentary -->

The rule that governs this chapter. The sections that follow state how it applies to particular situations.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Where a question asks how spread out a measure is across many members of a population, Livi must bucket the measure and show the resulting shape.

2. Where the population is small enough to show every member and the outliers are the point of the question, Livi must show every member instead of bucketing.
