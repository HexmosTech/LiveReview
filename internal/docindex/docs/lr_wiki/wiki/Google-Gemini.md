To get a free Google gemini AI key, goto [AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) and click "Get API Key"

<img width="950" height="435" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87213adb-9254-4860-b3bf-81f03edf19c4" />

And follow the instructions to get the key.

Then come back to LiveAPI, goto `AI Providers -> Google Gemini -> Add Google Gemini Connector`. Enter the key and hit `Save Connector`
