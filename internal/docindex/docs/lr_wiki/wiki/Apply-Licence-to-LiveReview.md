Purchase a LiveReview licence for unlimited lines of code reviews.

## Apply for a Licence

To get a licence, contact our support team through the [LiveReview Self-Hosted Access](https://hexmos.com/livereview/selfhosted-access/) page.

Once you receive your licence, apply it to your installation:

1. Click **Have a license?** in the ribbon at the top of the dashboard.
2. Paste the licence into the window and click **Save**.

## Manage Your Licence

You can always see the licence status in the ribbon at the top. To change the licence, click **Update License**, enter the new licence value and click **Save**.

Or, go to `Settings -> License`. You can do the same there, and also see more details such as the exact expiry date.

### Renew Your Licence

LiveReview shows a warning in the ribbon 15 days before your licence expires. To renew, contact support through the [LiveReview Self-Hosted Access](https://hexmos.com/livereview/selfhosted-access/) page, then apply the new licence with **Update License** as shown above.

If a licence expires, LiveReview keeps working but goes back to the Free 30k plan until a valid licence is applied.
