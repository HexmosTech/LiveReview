# Discord

Livi connects to Discord using a bot application you create in the Discord Developer Portal. You'll need two things to paste into LiveReview's **Settings → Integrations → Discord**: an **Application ID** and a **Bot Token**.

## Setup

1. Go to the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and click **New Application**, name it **Livi**, then click **Create**.

   Copy the **Application ID** shown on the **General Information** tab — you'll paste this into LiveReview's **Application ID** field.

2. Click **Bot** on the left sidebar.
3. Download the Livi icon below and upload it as the bot's avatar on this **Bot** page.

   <a
     href="/livereview/lrbot/lrbot-original.png"
     download="livi-icon.png"
     className="inline-flex items-center gap-2 px-4 py-2 rounded-lg text-sm font-medium bg-blue-600 text-white hover:bg-blue-700 dark:bg-blue-500 dark:hover:bg-blue-600 no-underline"
   >
     ⬇ Download Livi icon
   </a>

4. Under the **Privileged Gateway Intents** section, enable both of the following and click **Save Changes**:
   - **MESSAGE CONTENT INTENT** — required to read message content
   - **SERVER MEMBERS INTENT** — required to see guild members
5. Click **Reset Token**, then copy the bot token shown — you'll paste this into LiveReview's **Bot Token** field.
6. In your LiveReview instance, go to **Settings → Integrations → Discord**, paste the **Application ID** and **Bot Token**, and click **Save**.
7. After saving, click **Invite bot to your server** (shown once your Discord config is saved), choose your server, and authorize.

## Using Livi in Discord

### In a channel

Mention it: `@Livi Trigger a review for <PR URL>`.

### In a DM

Message the bot directly with any question.

If Livi doesn't respond, check with whoever manages your LiveReview instance that the server has picked up the saved Discord configuration.
